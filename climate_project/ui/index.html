Time & Urban Rhythm

These settings reflect whether the city is in a calmer period, a typical daytime phase, or a traffic-heavy window.

In Guided Forecast mode, the app uses the selected date and time, then fills the scientific parameters from typical historical Lahore conditions for that month and hour.

Atmosphere

Cold, moist air often helps smog linger. This panel lets you shape the moisture and temperature story.

Lower winter temperatures often strengthen trapping conditions, especially near dawn.

When dew point gets close to air temperature, haze and fog-like persistence become more likely.

Higher humidity can make polluted air persist and feel heavier.

High-pressure setups often align with calmer, more stagnant weather.

Airflow & Mixing

This panel controls how effectively Lahore can flush pollution out of near-surface air.

Stronger wind usually clears pollution faster. Weak wind lets emissions accumulate.

Northwesterly flow can align with regional smoke transport during the smog season.

Think of this as the vertical mixing room available for pollution. Low values trap smog near the ground.
